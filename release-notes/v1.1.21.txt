zh-CN:
ADB Manager v1.1.21

- 移除设备列表的常驻 3 秒轮询，避免设备控制台状态和诊断信息一直刷新。
- 诊断信息只会在选中的在线设备 serial 变化时重新获取，不再因为设备列表对象刷新而反复拉取。
- 保留启动、窗口重新聚焦、连接成功等必要时机的设备刷新。

en-US:
ADB Manager v1.1.21

- Removes continuous three-second device-list polling to stop device console status and diagnostics from refreshing constantly.
- Refreshes diagnostics only when the selected online device serial changes, instead of every device-list object refresh.
- Keeps device refreshes for startup, window refocus, and successful connection events.
