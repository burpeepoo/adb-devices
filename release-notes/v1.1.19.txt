zh-CN:
修复 App 内「重启 ADB」后仍然无法连接无线设备的问题。macOS 现在会优先使用本机已安装的 ADB（例如 Homebrew 或 Android SDK），避免把命令行已经恢复正常的 ADB server 又换回 App bundled ADB。
重启 ADB 后会等待 5037 端口真正恢复再继续扫描/重连，减少按钮显示完成但 server 尚未稳定的情况。

en-US:
Fixed wireless devices still failing to connect after using "Restart ADB" inside the app. On macOS, the app now prefers the host-installed ADB, such as Homebrew or Android SDK, before falling back to the bundled ADB.
After restarting ADB, the app waits for port 5037 to actually come back before scanning or reconnecting, reducing cases where the UI reports success before the server is stable.
