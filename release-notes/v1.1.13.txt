zh-CN:
无线调试连接失败时，“重启 ADB”现已升级为“修复无线配对”，会清理本机保存的无线配对记录并保留 ADB 身份密钥。
如果修复后仍无法重新发现或连接设备，界面才会显示单独的“重置本机 ADB 身份”按钮，并提示所有 Android 设备都需要重新授权或配对。

en-US:
When wireless debugging recovery is needed, Restart ADB now repairs wireless pairing state by clearing saved wireless pairing hosts while preserving the local ADB identity key.
If repair still cannot rediscover or reconnect the device, the UI now shows a separate Reset local ADB identity action with a warning that Android devices will need re-authorization or pairing.
