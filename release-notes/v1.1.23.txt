en-US:
Added a Remote Control app drawer for the selected device, so launchable apps can be opened directly from ADB Manager.
Drawer icons now parse from APK assets and load progressively in the background.
App icons are cached locally for faster repeat loads, with stale entries revalidated after 24 hours and rebuilt when APK paths change or caches age out.
Removed recent-app launch reordering so the drawer keeps a stable app-name order.

zh-CN:
远程控制新增应用抽屉，会根据当前选中的设备展示可启动 App，并可直接点击打开。
应用图标现在会从 APK 资源中解析，并在后台逐个加载，不阻塞应用列表展示。
App 图标已做本地缓存，重复进入会更快显示；超过 24 小时会后台校验，APK 路径变化或缓存过期时会重建。
移除了最近打开 App 的自动重排序，应用抽屉会保持稳定的应用名排序。
